# Falaknama Web

The public face of Falaknama (فلک‌نما), a Persian astrology product: a landing page that introduces it and, later, the web app where visitors read their own charts.

## Language

**Falaknama** (فلک‌نما):
The product and brand this site presents.
_Avoid_: Zayche (the reference site this design was inspired by, not our brand)

**Landing page** (صفحهٔ معرفی):
The single public page at the site root that introduces Falaknama and sends visitors on to the App.
_Avoid_: Homepage, home

**App** (اپلیکیشن):
The web application, on its own page, where a visitor enters birth details and reads their analyses. Every "ورود به اپلیکیشن" call to action leads here.
_Avoid_: Panel, dashboard (those mean the admin panel)

**Admin panel**:
The existing sign-in area for managing Users; not part of the visitor experience.
_Avoid_: App

**Article** (مقاله):
A piece of astrology writing with its own page under `/articles`, written and published from the Admin panel.
_Avoid_: Post, blog, news

**News item** (خبر):
A short, dated report of a sky happening read astrologically, such as a retrograde, an eclipse or a full moon in a sign. It has no page of its own: it shows on the Landing page until its date passes, and stays in the news archive until an admin removes it.
_Avoid_: Celestial event, astronomy news, اخبار اجرامی

**FAQ** (پرسش پرتکرار):
A question and its answer shown on the Landing page in an order the admins choose.
_Avoid_: Help, question

**Birth chart** (نقشهٔ تولد):
The positions of the planets, houses and aspects at the moment and place of a person's birth.
_Avoid_: Zayche, horoscope, natal chart (in Persian copy)
